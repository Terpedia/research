Research outline

One portfolio, several levels of evidence

Our work moves from literature discovery and chemical identity through receptor hypotheses, pathway inference, computational classification, and preparation-level biological evidence. Each project keeps its source, method, and uncertainty visible.

05 / Evidence rules

Claims stay smaller than the data.

A database record is not a validated natural product. A receptor association is not efficacy. A docking score is not affinity. A graph path is not proof of in-vivo production. We preserve those distinctions throughout the portfolio.
